Ranking Companion 1.2.2
25 November 2026

NEW
- Automatic game-component updates: matching, verified updates can download in the background and install once Live is closed.
- Game compatibility status and a manual check in Setup & data.
- Rankings from ended seasons are archived automatically, ready for the new season.

FIXED & IMPROVED
- Updated ranking and reward collection for the latest supported Live build.
- Fixed new-season rewards appearing as "Not checked" despite being available in F11.
- Game-component repairs now update the full ranking bundle together, with recovery if an update is interrupted.
- Saved accounts, rankings, rewards, history and preferences are preserved when updating.

The dungeon tracker is still in testing and is not included in this release.
